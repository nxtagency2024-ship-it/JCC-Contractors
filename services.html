Services

Utility Adjustments

Utility adjustments require skilled labor, hard work and attention to detail. JCC takes great pride in utilizing these attributes to provide a quality work product to our customers.

Utility Frame & Cover Adjustments

Typical utilities encountered

  • Water valve boxes
  • Gas valve boxes
  • Clean-out manholes
  • Storm drain / sanitary sewer manholes
  • Telecommunications manholes
  • Survey monument boxes

Collar Paving & Finishing

JCC has the capabilities to finish the utility frame collars to final grade with a lift of asphalt concrete or finished concrete with additions such as lamp black coloring and broom finishes.

Relevant utility categories

Survey Monument Installations

Survey monuments located within project limits can be disturbed, destroyed or non conforming to current specifications. JCC has the experience and skills to work with your surveyor to install monument cores as required.

GPS Utility Lid Referencing

JCC has the capability to reference accurately the location of utility frames and covers prior to paving operations.

Don't see your utility type?

If you do not see a utility category listed above that we cover, please contact us to discuss further your utility adjustment needs.
