About

Johnson Construction Company

JCC Incorporated is a utility adjustment contractor.

Company overview

Johnson Construction Company, operating as JCC Incorporated, specializes in utility adjustments. Our work centers on bringing utility frames, covers, and related structures to final grade so they are safe, accessible, and accurately located.

Specialization

Utility adjustments require skilled labor, hard work and attention to detail. JCC takes great pride in utilizing these attributes to provide a quality work product to our customers.

Experience and capabilities

  • Utility frame and cover adjustments for water, gas, sewer, storm drain, and telecommunications structures.
  • Survey monument installations, working with your surveyor to install monument cores as required.
  • GPS utility lid referencing before paving operations.
  • Collar paving and finishing to final grade.

In 2025, JCC adjusted 12,500 utility frames and covers for 23 general contractors and municipalities across 32 cities.
